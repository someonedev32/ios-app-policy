Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06

This Privacy Policy describes how DocuScan Pro (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to work offline for its core document scanning and editing features. Documents and scanned content remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them. If you use in-app purchases or subscriptions, limited purchase-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as described below. If you have questions, contact us at info@studiopixel.ch.

1) Data Collection

We do not collect your documents or personal information through the App's core scanning features.

The App does not require an account and does not upload scanned documents, images, or extracted text to our servers. Core features are designed to work offline.

If you make an in-app purchase or subscribe to a paid feature, Apple and RevenueCat may process limited purchase-related information to complete, manage, and verify the purchase. See Section 6.

2) On-Device Processing

All core features—including document scanning, image enhancement, cropping, adding text or signatures, PDF creation/export, and text recognition (OCR)—are performed entirely on your device.

No documents, images, or extracted text are uploaded to our servers. Internet access is not required for the App's core scanning and document processing functionality.

3) Permissions

The App may request the following permissions only to provide functionality:

  • Camera: to scan documents.
  • Photos: to import images or save exported files, when you choose to use these features.

Permission access is used locally on your device. You can manage permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4) Storage

Scans, processed documents, and exported files are stored locally on your device. You can delete your documents at any time within the App or by removing the App from your device.

Files that you choose to export, share, or save outside the App may remain in the destination you selected and must be managed separately.

5) Tracking & Advertising

The App does not track users, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

The App does not include third-party advertising networks or analytics services.

6) Purchases and RevenueCat

The App may offer paid features, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. If you use these features, purchase-related information may be processed by the following services:

  • Apple: Apple processes App Store payments and provides purchase or subscription information needed to complete transactions and verify purchase status. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat helps manage and verify purchases, subscriptions, and access to paid features. It may process purchase-related information such as an anonymous app user identifier, product or entitlement information, transaction status, and subscription status.

Purchase-related information is used for purchase management, entitlement verification, subscription status, and related support. RevenueCat is not used to process or store your scanned documents, images, or OCR text.

Purchase processing may require an internet connection even though the App's core scanning and document processing features work offline. Purchase availability and verification may depend on Apple and RevenueCat services.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms. For more information, please review Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

7) Third-Party Sharing

The App does not share your documents or scanned content with third parties by default.

If you choose to export or share a file using iOS sharing options, the destination app or service will handle that file under its own privacy policy. We do not control how the destination service processes or stores files you choose to share.

RevenueCat may receive purchase-related information as described in Section 6, but it does not receive your scanned documents through the App's core scanning functionality.

8) Data Retention and Deletion

Documents and scans are stored locally on your device. You control how long they remain there and can delete them within the App.

Removing the App deletes its locally stored data from the device, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you have created. Exported files and copies stored outside the App may need to be deleted separately.

Purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat may remain subject to their respective retention practices and legal or transaction requirements.

9) Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

10) Security

The App is designed to minimize data exposure by processing documents locally and avoiding uploads to our servers.

The security of your device, including its passcode, operating system protections, backups, and any apps you share data with, also impacts the security of your documents.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11) Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the effective date above. The updated policy will be posted on this page.

12) Contact

For questions about privacy, contact: info@studiopixel.ch
